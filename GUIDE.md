# SpotBuddy User Guide

SpotBuddy helps kitesurfers find out who's at the water, plan sessions, and connect with their kite crew.

---

## Getting started

1. Open the app and create an account with your email address.
2. Set a display name and profile photo.
3. Go to your **profile** (top-right avatar) and set your **nationality** and **skill level** (1–5 stars). This helps other riders know who you are.

---

## Home screen

The home screen shows your spots and what's happening today.

- **Nearest spot** — your closest kite spot with live wind and how many riders are there. Tap it to open the spot page. If you're within range, a **Check in** button appears.
- **My Spots** — your saved spots with live wind, rider count, and quick access to check in or plan a session.
- **Planned session** — if you have a session planned for today, it shows here with the time and spot.

Switch between **Today** and **Tomorrow** using the tabs at the top.

---

## Spot page

Tap any spot to open its detail page.

### Checking in
Press **Check in** to mark yourself as live at the spot. You need to be within 500m of the spot. Your session will grow dynamically on the timeline until you check out.

### Planning a session
Press **Plan a session** to set a time window for today or tomorrow. Other riders can see you're planning to go and can join your session.

### The timeline
The timeline shows all sessions for the day. Live sessions grow in real time. Tap a session bar to see details or join.

### Joining a session
You can join someone's session if you are their buddy (or a buddy of someone already in the group). Tap **Join** on their session bar.

### Rating the spot
After checking in, press **Rate the spot** to share current conditions:
- **Wind** — speed in knots
- **Direction** — wind direction relative to the shore
- **Water** — water conditions
- **Crowd** — how busy it is

Ratings are visible to everyone on the spot page and are time-weighted so recent ratings count most.

### Checking out
Press **Check out** when you're done. Your session is marked as finished.

---

## Discover

Tap the compass icon to open the **Discover map**. All known spots are shown as dots — brighter colours mean more activity.

- **Search** — type a spot name to find it. Use **Show on map** to fly to it, or **Open →** to go straight to the spot page.
- **Suggest a spot** — if you're standing at a spot that isn't listed yet, tap **Suggest a spot**, enter a name, and submit. Your suggestion is reviewed before it goes live.

---

## Buddies

Tap the people icon to open the **Buddies** screen.

- **Follow** someone by finding them and sending a follow request. Once they follow you back, you're buddies.
- **Buddy benefits**: you can see each other's sessions and join each other's groups.
- Tap any avatar on a session or spot page to view a rider's profile, send them a **direct message**, or send a **buddy request**.

---

## Chat

- **Direct messages** — tap a buddy's avatar anywhere in the app to open a chat.
- **Group chat** — when multiple riders are in the same session, a **Group Chat** button appears. Everyone in the group can message here.
- **Broadcast** — send a message to all your buddies at once from the chat screen.

---

## Notifications

SpotBuddy sends push notifications for:
- A buddy checks in at one of your spots
- Someone joins your session
- A new buddy request or acceptance
- A direct message
- A broadcast from a buddy

You can turn off direct message notifications in your **profile settings**.

---

## Profile

Tap your avatar in the top right to open your profile.

- **Display name** — visible to all other riders.
- **Profile photo** — tap to change.
- **Nationality** — shown as a flag on your avatar.
- **Skill level** — 1 (beginner) to 5 (pro). Shown as filled dots on your avatar.
- **DM notifications** — toggle push notifications for direct messages.

---

## Tips

- Add your regular spots to **My Spots** from the spot page so they always appear on your home screen.
- The **wind widget** on each spot uses live Open-Meteo data and updates automatically.
- Session bars on the timeline are colour-coded: green = live, blue = going, grey = maybe.
- If you arrive at a spot earlier than your planned time, check in anyway — your session bar will adjust.
